This rubric provides an objective, quantitative system to evaluate and score trendline pullback trade setups. It integrates classical technical analysis, market geometry, and Smart Money Concepts (SMC / ICT).

## 📋 Detailed Scoring Rubric Breakdown

### 1. Higher Timeframe (HTF) & Structure — (25 Points Max)

#### A. HTF Trend Alignment (+15 Pts)
* **`15 Pts` — Strict Alignment:** The lower-timeframe (LTF) setup direction strictly matches the H4 and Daily Market Structure. Price is making consecutive Higher Highs / Higher Lows (for longs) or Lower Highs / Lower Lows (for shorts), and the HTF Market Structure Shift (MSS) is intact.
* **`0 Pts` — Counter-Trend or Undefined:** The trade is taken against the H4/Daily order flow, or the HTF is in an aggressive multi-leg counter-trend expansion.
  > 🚫 **Hard Rule:** A score of `0` in this sub-item results in an **Instant Disqualification** for the trade setup regardless of total points.

#### B. HTF Liquidity Draw (+10 Pts)
* **`10 Pts` — Clear Draw Present:** There is an unmitigated HTF liquidity pool acting as a magnet for price. Examples include:
  * Equal Highs / Equal Lows (EQH / EQL) on H4/Daily.
  * Unmitigated HTF Fair Value Gap (FVG) or Liquidity Void.
  * Major untapped Daily/Weekly Swing High or Swing Low.
* **`5 Pts` — Intermediate Target:** The draw on liquidity is on a mid-tier timeframe (e.g., H1/H4 local swing point) with limited expansion space.
* **`0 Pts` — No Draw / Running into HTF Obstacle:** Price is trading directly into a major HTF key support/resistance zone, leaving an unfavorable Risk-to-Reward ratio ($\text{RR} < 1:2$).

### 2. Trendline Geometry & Spacing — (15 Points Max)

```text
               (Point 3 - Entry Zone)
                     /\    
  (Point 1)         /  \   
     /\     (Point 2)   \  
    /  \       /\        \ 
___/____\_____/____\______\_________________ Dynamic Trendline
   |<- 10-20+ ->|<- 10-20+ ->|  (Candle Spacing)
```

#### A. Valid Touch Count (+5 Pts)
* **`5 Pts` — Established Line ($\ge 3$ Touches):** The trendline has at least **3 distinct, clean structural swing touches** prior to the current setup. Point 1 establishes the line, Point 2 confirms it, and Point 3+ serves as the execution entry zone.
* **`2 Pts` — Provisional Line ($2$ Touches):** The line connects only 2 previous points. The current touch is only the 3rd touch attempting confirmation.
* **`0 Pts` — Single Touch / Arbitrary Line:** Drawn across wicks without structural points.

#### B. Optimal Slope Angle (+5 Pts)
* **`5 Pts` — Sustainable Angle ($30^\circ \text{ to } 45^\circ$):** The line reflects sustainable market momentum. The price trends steadily without parabolic acceleration or prolonged sideways stagnation.
* **`0 Pts` — Extreme Angle ($>60^\circ \text{ or } <20^\circ$):**
  * **$> 60^\circ$ (Too Steep):** Represents parabolic blow-off moves prone to immediate, aggressive structural failure.
  * **$< 20^\circ$ (Too Flat):** Reflects a weak trend or broad consolidation where trendline boundaries are frequently violated by random market noise.

#### C. Candle Spacing Between Touches (+5 Pts)
* **`5 Pts` — Well-Spaced Swings ($\ge 10\text{--}20+$ Candles):** Distinct structural swings separated by significant time and price movement ($10$ to $20+$ candles apart depending on timeframe).
* **`0 Pts` — Clustered Touches ($< 5$ Candles):** Touches occur within a single tight consolidation range rather than across separate structural impulses.

### 3. Pullback Dynamics & Price Action — (15 Points Max)

#### A. Decaying Counter-Trend Momentum (+10 Pts)
* **`10 Pts` — Order Flow Decay:** As price approaches the trendline, counter-trend candles exhibit:
  * Progressively smaller real bodies (range contraction).
  * Decreasing volume (indicating a lack of institutional selling/buying pressure).
  * Wicks forming on the side of the trendline (showing early absorption).
* **`0 Pts` — Aggressive Counter-Trend Impulse:** Price approaches the trendline via wide-range, multi-standard-deviation expansion candles with heavy volume. This indicates an active institutional attempt to break market structure.

#### B. Trigger Candle Rejection Signal (+5 Pts)
* **`5 Pts` — Explicit Rejection Confirmation:** The contact candle produces a distinct price action signal directly on or wicking through the trendline:
  * **Pinbar / Hammer / Shooting Star:** Rejection wick measures at least $60\%$ of the candle's total range.
  * **Engulfing Pattern:** Full body close covering the preceding counter-trend candle.
  * **Railroad Tracks / Morning-Evening Star:** Clean multi-candle reversal at the contact zone.
* **`0 Pts` — Neutral / Breakdown Close:** A full-bodied candle closes cleanly beyond the trendline with no rejection wick.

### 4. Classical Technical Confluence — (20 Points Max)

#### A. Horizontal Support/Resistance Flip (+8 Pts)
* **Criteria:** The trendline touchpoint intersects directly with a horizontal **Role Reversal Zone** (a former swing high that now acts as support in an uptrend, or a former swing low acting as resistance in a downtrend).

#### B. Fibonacci Retracement Alignment (+6 Pts)
* **Criteria:** The trendline intersection aligns with a key Fibonacci level measured from the impulse swing low to swing high:
  * **Full 6 Pts:** Intersection sits inside the **Golden Pocket ($61.8\% \text{ to } 78.6\%$)**.
  * **3 Pts:** Intersection aligns with the $50\%$ equilibrium level.

#### C. Dynamic Moving Average / VWAP Alignment (+6 Pts)
* **Criteria:** The trendline touchpoint coincides with a key dynamic indicator:
  * **Exponential Moving Averages:** 20 EMA (momentum) or 50 SMA (structural trend).
  * **Anchor VWAP / Session VWAP:** Price touches the trendline and VWAP line within the same candle boundary.

### 5. ICT / SMC Order Flow Elements — (25 Points Max)
*(Select all applicable items; maximum category cap = 25 Pts)*

#### A. Liquidity Sweep / Turtle Soup (+10 Pts)
* **Criteria:** Price expands through the trendline or an immediate local swing point to run retail stop-loss clusters (engineering liquidity), then violently reverses to close back above/below the trendline in the direction of the main trend.

#### B. Fair Value Gap / Imbalance (+8 Pts)
* **Criteria:** An unmitigated **3-candle Fair Value Gap (FVG)** or Imbalance zone overlaps with the trendline contact point, offering institutional re-pricing efficiency before expansion.

#### C. Institutional Blocks (+7 Pts)
* **Criteria:** The trendline touchpoint aligns with an active institutional block:
  * **Breaker Block:** A failed order block that was broken during a liquidity sweep and now acts as support/resistance.
  * **Mitigation Block:** An order block formed during an unsuccessful attempt to create a higher high/lower low.
  * **Rejection Block:** The long upper/lower wick zone of a high-timeframe candle where aggressive limit orders reside.

---

### 6. Special Pattern Mechanics: Descending Triangle Retest (+5 Bonus Pts)

#### Descending Triangle Breakdown / Retest (+5 Pts)
* **Bearish Continuation Setup:** Price has broken down below flat horizontal support in a Descending Triangle. The pullback retests the horizontal support-turned-resistance line at the exact intersection of the descending hypotenuse trendline (Apex Confluence).
* **Bullish Reversal / Breakout Setup:** Price expands out of the descending hypotenuse trendline, then pulls back to test the top of the broken trendline as dynamic support.

## 🚫 Instant Disqualification Rules (Hard Red Flags)

If **ANY** of the following conditions exist, the setup score is automatically overridden to **0 Points** and execution is forbidden:

1. **Counter HTF Trend:** Trading against H4 or Daily market structure.
2. **Aggressive Structural Breakdown:** A candle closes fully past the trendline with a large body before any rejection occurs.
3. **Impaired Risk/Reward:** Target liquidity is too close, resulting in an expected $\text{Risk-to-Reward Ratio} < 1:2$.
4. **High-Impact News Event:** The entry occurs within 15 minutes before or after high-impact economic news releases (e.g., NFP, CPI, FOMC).
