5. Check understanding

Closed-book question: A directional move has five visible turns but wave 4 overlaps wave 1 in a proposed standard impulse. Must you classify it as a valid impulse?

Answer key: open after writing your answer

No. The proposed standard impulse fails its overlap rule; consider another supported structure or mark it unclear.

Use both the exact rule screen and contextual human review. No-violation-detected is not a verified count or a pass for the whole unit.
